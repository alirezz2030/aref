# Data Analyst Interview Case Template

## 1. Clarify the decision
- What decision is the business trying to make?
- What outcome and time period matter?
- Who is included or excluded?

## 2. Validate data and metric definitions
- What is one row?
- Are keys unique where expected?
- What is missing, duplicated, delayed, or inconsistent?
- How is the KPI defined?

## 3. Decompose the business metric
Write the formula or driver tree. Example: Revenue = Orders × Average Order Value.

## 4. Segment and compare
Compare suitable periods, products, regions, channels, customer types, or cohorts. Use consistent denominators.

## 5. Test competing explanations
Separate observed patterns from causes. Identify what additional data or experiment would help distinguish explanations.

## 6. Quantify impact and uncertainty
Estimate the magnitude, explain uncertainty, and state assumptions. Do not rely on a p-value alone.
